No form to fill. Claude splits your note into separate claims and tags how well each one is known, then you correct it before anything is saved. Nothing reaches a card until you approve it.

Teaching about one of our own products? Choose Impact Analytics and the product. That becomes the ground truth for our side of every card: the mechanism, the advantages and the proof points. A card may only claim what is taught here.

The file is read once and thrown away. Nothing is stored except the claims you approve, so a document under an agreement never lands on a disk here. Reading it does send its text to the Anthropic API, so upload what you are allowed to share, and take the same care you would with any competitor material.
